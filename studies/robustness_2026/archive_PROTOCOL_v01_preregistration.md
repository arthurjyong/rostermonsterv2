# Prospective Robustness Study Protocol (pre-registration draft)

_Status: DRAFT v0.1, 2026-07-04. Generated from the planning workflow (session 1b31c7ab, run wf_4b95a1c6-c59); edit freely, this is not yet frozen. The freeze commit (target: late July 2026, tag `robustness-freeze-2026-08`) is the pre-registration event._

## 1. Design in one paragraph

Temporal development/confirmatory split. Development set: the archived May and June 2026 month-cycles (collected ad hoc during live tuning; used only to fix the production tuple, the N=5 tuning protocol, and the hypotheses below). Confirmatory set: every unit-month from August through December 2026 with a published roster, collected under this frozen protocol. Production solves are never altered or influenced; all confirmatory experiments are offline recomputation on each month's archived snapshot. All confirmatory endpoints are computed under a frozen reference scoring config via deterministic re-scoring, so operator weight retuning cannot contaminate them.

## 2. Hypotheses

### H1-tuple-transfer (primary)

The production tuple (L=50, idleThreshold=3500, swapProbability=0.5), fixed on May/June 2026 development months, remains within the equivalence margin of the per-month full-sweep optimum on every unseen confirmatory month: for each confirmatory month, the mean bestScore of 30 offline seeds at the production tuple is statistically equivalent to the mean bestScore of 30 seeds at that month's best-performing swept tuple, all scored under the frozen reference scoring config.

**Endpoint:** Per month: TOST equivalence on per-seed bestScore (production tuple vs per-month sweep optimum), margin delta = +/-2.0 score points, alpha=0.05, paired on shared seed list, two one-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank tests (paired t as sensitivity check). Across-month success criterion: equivalence declared in >= 4 of 5 month-instances (>= 8 of 10 if two units accrue). DISCONFIRMABLE PREDICTION: if in >= 2 month-instances the sweep optimum is BOTH non-equivalent (TOST fails) AND superior (one-sided Wilcoxon p < 0.01 with median advantage > 2.0 points), transferability is falsified and per-month re-tuning is declared necessary; this outcome would be reported as the headline result, not buried.

### H2-plateau-cliff (primary)

The L-response shape found on development months (plateau across L in {1,10,50,100}, cliff at L >= 500) reproduces on each unseen month.

**Endpoint:** Per month, 30 seeds per L level over L in {1,10,50,100,500,1000}: (a) plateau: every pair within {1,10,50,100} passes TOST at delta = +/-2.0, alpha=0.05; (b) cliff: L=500 and L=1000 each worse than L=50 by one-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank p < 0.01 with median deficit > 2.0 points. Across-month criterion: both (a) and (b) hold in >= 4 of 5 months. Falsifier: cliff absent (L=1000 TOST-equivalent to L=50) in >= 2 months.

### H3-bestofk-saturation (secondary)

Best-of-K saturation at K=10-20 (FW-0036) reproduces: on each unseen month, K=10 captures >= 95% of the achievable best-of-K gain.

**Endpoint:** Per month, from the 30-seed pool at the production tuple: bootstrap best-of-k curve (10,000 resamples without replacement); endpoint is E[best-of-10 minus best-of-1] / E[best-of-30 minus best-of-1] >= 0.95, with bootstrap 95% CI lower bound >= 0.90. Across-month criterion: >= 4 of 5 months.

### H4-srb-dominance (secondary)

LAHC's matched-wall-time-budget dominance over SEEDED_RANDOM_BLIND (dev result: +53.85 mean points, 5/5 wins) replicates on unseen months.

**Endpoint:** Per month: 5 paired master seeds, SRB at the wall-time-matched K (recomputed per month from measured per-trajectory wall time, dev anchor SRB-K=100 vs LAHC-K=10) vs LAHC at production tuple; endpoints are (a) 5/5 LAHC wins on bestScore under the frozen reference config, and (b) paired one-sided Wilcoxon/t p < 0.05. Across-month criterion: (a) holds in 5 of 5 months. Effect size reported per month; magnitude is exploratory, direction is confirmatory.

### H5-cheap-tune-validity (secondary)

The cheap N=5-seed tune-then-run protocol (FW-0037) picks a tuple on unseen months that is equivalent to the 30-seed full-sweep optimum, i.e. 5 seeds are enough to tune in production.

**Endpoint:** Per month, computed by bootstrap subsampling of the existing 30-seed sweep data (no extra compute): draw 10,000 random 5-seed subsets, record the tuple each subset would pick (argmax of subset mean); endpoints are (a) pick-stability: the picked tuple lies in the true plateau set (tuples TOST-equivalent to the 30-seed optimum) in >= 90% of subsets, and (b) regret: mean bestScore shortfall of the picked tuple vs the 30-seed optimum <= 2.0 points in >= 95% of subsets. Across-month criterion: >= 4 of 5 months.

### H6-idle-elbow-and-joint (exploratory)

The idleThreshold elbow sits in [2000, 5000] on each unseen month, and there is no material L-x-idleThreshold interaction near the production tuple (the joint pair was never swept together on development data; the July control experiment addresses this on dev months and this hypothesis extends it prospectively).

**Endpoint:** Per month, 10 seeds per cell over the joint mini-grid L in {10,50,100} x idleThreshold in {2000,3500,5000}: (a) elbow location from the marginal idle curve falls in [2000,5000]; (b) interaction: two-way rank-based ANOVA (aligned rank transform) interaction term p > 0.05 AND all 9 cell means within 2.0 points of the production-tuple cell. No across-month replication threshold pre-set; reported descriptively.

### H7-wall-time-stability (exploratory)

Per-trajectory wall time at the production tuple is stable enough across months (coefficient of variation of monthly medians < 50%) that wall-time-matched budget comparisons (H4) and the 10-minute async cap (D-0071) remain valid.

**Endpoint:** Descriptive: median and IQR of per-trajectory wallTimeSeconds (new result.json field) per month, plus offline-sweep wall times; no formal test; flagged if any month's median departs > 2x from the dev anchor ~16s/trajectory local.

## 3. Per-month procedure

0. Step 0 (continuous, operators, no change): production solves run exactly as today, async cloud LAHC at the hardcoded production tuple with production K; operators retune componentWeightRecords/callPointRecords in the Scorer Config tab freely; nothing about the study is visible to them and no extra clicks are asked of them. The pilot IS normal operations.
1. Step 1 (month close + <= 7 days, maintainer, ~15 min): month-end export. (a) Run the routine gsutil rsync of the GCS bucket to '/Volumes/Acer FA200 4TB/Work/Programming/rostermonster-ops/gcs_archive/rostermonsterv2-lahc' so every snapshot.json + result.json pair for the month is locally archived. (b) Export the final published roster tab of the month's spreadsheet as a values-only CSV into the ops archive (enables derived chosen-candidate identification and post-publication-edit Hamming distance against candidates[].assignments, no code needed). (c) Fill the ops log row(s) for the month (date, operator role, re-solve reason if known, candidate adopted if known, maintainer minutes).
2. Step 2 (same session, maintainer, ~10 min): canonical instance selection by pre-registered rule: the confirmatory instance for month M is the snapshot.json behind the production run whose output the operators actually published (identified by matching the published roster CSV against candidates[].assignments across the month's runIds; ties broken by latest generationTimestamp). If no run matches (heavy manual editing), fall back to the latest OK-state production snapshot of the month and record the discrepancy. One instance per unit per month.
3. Step 3 (within 14 days of month close, maintainer laptop or Cloud Batch, ~3-5 wall-clock hours on 8 cores per instance): offline confirmatory sweeps against the archived snapshot.json using the FROZEN scripts at the tagged SHA with the pre-registered 30-seed master list. Runs: (a) L-sweep, 6 levels x 30 seeds = 180 trajectories (~16s each local, ~48 CPU-min at 8-way parallel per level batch); (b) joint mini-grid 3x3 x 10 seeds = 90 trajectories minus cells shared with (a); (c) SRB paired comparison, 5 master seeds at the wall-time-matched K (uses committed descendant of experimental/m6_lahc_comparison/comparable_budget.py and paired_comparison.py); (d) best-of-K and N=5 tuning endpoints need no new runs (bootstrap over (a)'s 30-seed pool, descendant of seed_count_analysis.py and best_of_k_analysis.py). Total roughly 300 trajectories, ~2.5 CPU-hours single-thread-equivalent x safety factor, well under an afternoon.
4. Step 4 (same session): deterministic re-scoring. Every trajectory's assignments are re-scored under the frozen reference scoring config (committed reference_scoring_config.json holding a fixed componentWeightRecords + callPointRecords set) via the pure scorer function; the month's LIVE config (already embedded in snapshot.json scoringConfigRecords) is recorded descriptively alongside. All H1-H6 endpoints use reference-config scores only.
5. Step 5 (same week, maintainer, ~1 hour): run the frozen analysis script (one entrypoint, e.g. studies/robustness_2026/analyze_month.py at the tagged SHA) which emits per-month results JSON + PDF plots + a filled hypothesis-outcome row (H1..H7 pass/fail per pre-registered endpoint). No test, margin, or seed may be altered; any deviation is an amendment logged with date and rationale in studies/robustness_2026/AMENDMENTS.md before the next month's analysis.
6. Step 6: commit. Per-month results JSON, PDFs, the hypothesis register row, sha256 hashes of the raw snapshot.json/result.json inputs, and the ops-log row go into the repo under studies/robustness_2026/ (a deliberately COMMITTED directory, breaking the experimental-stays-uncommitted convention because the protocol requires an auditable timestamped trail); raw archives stay in the ops GCS mirror. Push to GitHub so commit timestamps serve as the pre-registration audit trail.
7. Step 7 (rolling): update the running results table in the paper draft; do NOT recompute earlier months under any changed code; earlier months are final once committed.

## 5. Frozen elements (declared at the freeze commit)

- Production tuple L=50 / idleThreshold=3500 / swapProbability=0.5, declared as-deployed (hardcoded in the M7 worker path per D-0070), identified by worker container image digest and repo SHA in the protocol doc; it must not be retuned during the window (any emergency change ends the window and is an amendment).
- Sweep grid and seeds: L levels {1,10,50,100,500,1000}; joint grid L x idle = {10,50,100} x {2000,3500,5000}; swapProbability held at 0.5 in confirmatory sweeps (move-mix plateau is a dev-set finding, cited not re-swept); the 30-seed master list and the 5 SRB paired master seeds, written verbatim into PROTOCOL.md.
- Statistical machinery: TOST margin delta = +/-2.0 score points, alpha = 0.05, Wilcoxon signed-rank primary with paired t sensitivity, bootstrap resample count 10,000, all replication thresholds (>=4/5 etc.) as stated in the hypotheses, committed before the window opens.
- Analysis code: studies/robustness_2026/ scripts + analyze_month.py + reference_scoring_config.json + PROTOCOL.md committed and git-tagged (robustness-freeze-2026-08) and pushed to GitHub before the first August export; the public commit timestamp is the pre-registration evidence. Deviations only via dated entries in AMENDMENTS.md written before the affected month is analyzed.
- Reference scoring config: the exact componentWeightRecords (9 components) + callPointRecords (58 rows) set in force at freeze, committed as reference_scoring_config.json. ALL confirmatory endpoints (H1-H6) are computed by deterministic re-scoring of trajectory assignments under this config only.
- Operator weight-change rule (the clean separation): operators stay fully free to retune weights and call points at any time, because it is their roster and the pilot must remain uninfluenced. Every production snapshot.json already embeds the live scoringConfigRecords, so each month's live config is captured automatically at zero cost. The analysis NEVER pools live-config scores across runs or months; live-config scores appear only descriptively (a per-month config-history table in the paper, plus an exploratory sensitivity appendix re-running the H1 endpoint under each month's own live config). Solver-robustness claims are therefore invariant to weight churn by construction. Exception clause: a change to the scoring FORMULATION itself (new component, FW-0033-style rework) cannot be absorbed by re-weighting; it triggers an amendment and splits the window into before/after strata analyzed separately.
- Canonical-instance selection rule (Step 2 of the monthly procedure), fixed in advance so the maintainer cannot cherry-pick favorable snapshots.
- Month inclusion rule: every unit-month from August through December 2026 with a published roster enters; a paused unit-month is recorded as missing, never replaced by a re-run; minimum evaluable set for the primary claim is pre-declared as 4 unit-month instances.
- Production K and quota are explicitly NOT frozen (ops may change them); all confirmatory endpoints use offline 30-seed pools and bootstrap best-of-k, so production K changes cannot contaminate them; production K per run is reported descriptively from result.json kApproved.

## 6. Statistical power, honestly

Two statistical layers with very different power, and the paper must not blur them. WITHIN-MONTH (well powered): each endpoint rests on 30 seeds per cell. Dev-set data (May means 90.2-91.4 across the plateau, per-seed sd small relative to the 2-point margin; the existing seed_count_analysis showed 100% Wilcoxon power at N>=5 for cliff-sized effects and 93.2% pick-stability at N=5) implies N=30 gives ample power both to DECLARE equivalence inside +/-2 (TOST power depends on sd/margin; with the observed sd of roughly 0.5-1.5 points, N=30 paired gives power well above 0.9 for a true difference of 0) and to DETECT the cliff (dev cliff effects were tens of points, p<<0.001 at N=5). So each month yields an essentially decisive per-month verdict; within-month power is not the constraint. ACROSS-MONTH (the honest limit): 5 monthly cycles, at best ~10 unit-month instances, and the two units share the same hospital, template (cgh_icu_hd), doctor pool overlap, request culture, and holiday calendar, so instances are positively correlated, not independent draws from 'rostering problems'. Therefore cross-month claims are stated as replication proportions with exact binomial intervals, never pooled p-values or meta-analysis: '5/5 months replicated' has a 95% Clopper-Pearson interval of about [0.48, 1.00], which is the honest strength of the evidence, and the paper will print that interval. What CAN be claimed: the dev-tuned production tuple and the N=5 tuning protocol transferred without degradation to every (or k of n) consecutive unseen month at this deployment, under a pre-registered protocol, which is exactly the 'robust in production' question and is more than the current single-fixture literature norm for LAHC. What CANNOT be claimed: generalization to other hospitals, other roster templates, other scales (the shapes span only 18-22 doctors x 27-31 days x ~480-650 requests), other scoring formulations, or LAHC in general; nor that the tuple is OPTIMAL for future months (equivalence within 2 points, not superiority); nor anything causal about operator behavior. If months turn out too similar (shape stats nearly identical), the robustness claim weakens toward 'stable under natural monthly perturbation' and the paper must characterize the perturbation magnitude (request-set turnover, doctor-roster turnover, holiday structure per month) in a dedicated table so reviewers can judge it. A null-variation window is itself a risk to the paper's interest, listed under risks.

## 8. Risks and mitigations

- Pilot pauses for a month or a unit drops out: the window shrinks below 5 instances. Mitigation: pre-declared minimum evaluable set of 4 unit-months; two units give slack; if fewer than 4 accrue by Dec, the paper reframes the confirmatory window as 'ongoing' and reports what accrued with the pre-registered protocol as the contribution.
- Operator weight configs churn (June saw 4 configs in one month): fully absorbed by the frozen reference re-scoring design; residual risk is a scoring FORMULATION change (FW-0033) mid-window, which would stratify the window; mitigation is simply deferring FW-0033 work until after December.
- Production K or quota changes (FW entry already anticipates K=88 vs 176 quota moves): does not touch confirmatory endpoints (offline 30-seed pools, bootstrap best-of-k), but changes production wall-time telemetry; handled descriptively via the new kApproved + wall-time echo.
- Worker code changes or hotfixes during the window alter the solver path: determinism contract (byte-identical, section 12A.4) means old behavior is reproducible from the tagged SHA, but a solver hotfix on the LIVE path is an amendment; mitigation: freeze the solver-relevant code path in practice (only UX/shim changes during the window), and record the image digest per run via the new echo field.
- December holiday-map problem: Dec 2026 sheet generation needs 2027 holidays entered around mid-November; if missed, the Dec instance is lost or delayed past the submission cutoff. Mitigation: explicit Nov checklist item plus calendar reminder.
- Fixture shapes too similar across months (same template, overlapping doctors, 18-22 x 27-31 shapes): robustness claim risks looking trivial. Mitigation: per-month perturbation table (request turnover %, doctor turnover, holiday structure, prefilled assignments) to quantify how different the months actually are; if truly near-identical, reframe honestly as stability under natural monthly perturbation and lean harder on the joint-sweep + cliff-reproduction content.
- MISTA deadline earlier than expected (assumed late Jan 2027, could be early Jan): mitigation is the Jan-10 submittable-draft target, with December's instance as the only content at risk of being dropped.
- Archive loss: Cloud Logging [timing] markers expire ~30 days and GCS bucket lifecycle or manual cleanup could eat run artifacts; mitigation: the logging sink and the monthly rsync + sha256 manifest, both landing before August.
- Snapshot privacy: snapshot.json carries real doctor display names, so raw confirmatory data can never be committed to the public repo (the May 2026 history rewrite already burned this lesson); mitigation: commit hashes + anonymized derived tables only, keep raw files in the ops mirror, and build the anonymization step into analyze_month.py from day one.
- Published roster matches NO solver candidate (heavy manual editing) in some month: canonical-instance fallback rule covers instance selection, but a high Hamming distance month weakens the deployed-system narrative; report it honestly, it is signal for paper two.
- Compute environment drift for offline sweeps (new laptop, macOS update, Python bump): determinism contract should hold byte-identical results, but pin the Python version + lockfile in studies/robustness_2026/ and re-verify one May fixture run reproduces its archived bestScore at the start of every month's analysis as a canary.
- The disconfirming outcome itself: the tuple fails to transfer in 2+ months. This is a paper risk only if treated as failure; the protocol pre-commits to publishing it as the finding (per-month re-tuning necessary, N=5 protocol becomes the story), which MISTA reviewers will likely value as much as a confirmation.
